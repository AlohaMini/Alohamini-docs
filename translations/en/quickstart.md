:::{admonition} Not sure which model you have?
:class: am-extra

- **Standard 2**: a 3D printed chassis with STS3215 wheel servos.
- **2 Pro**: a reinforced metal chassis with STS3250 wheel servos.

Check the device labels and kit list. Modified robots need their configuration checked individually; appearance alone is not enough.
:::
