(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u665269cf)=

In recent years, artificial intelligence, programming and robotics education have gradually entered primary and secondary school classrooms, clubs and scientific and technological activities. Through graphical programming, intelligent hardware construction, robot control and project practice, students begin to understand how algorithms, sensors and automation technologies affect real life.

The Ministry of Education announced a total of 47 national competitions for primary and secondary school students in the 2025-2028 school year. Among them, natural science literacy competitions have covered many directions such as artificial intelligence, robots, drones, inventions and creations. ## 3. AlohaMini Product Description

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u49e20b9c)=

**AlohaMini is a two-arm movable lifting robot designed for embodied intelligent science education, student project research, club activities and competition practice.**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-2c199ae5)=

### Hardware configuration: What tasks can the Aloha Mini do?

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-uce1d93d1)=

AlohaMini integrates **movement, lifting, dual-arm operation and visual perception capabilities** and can support students to design, train and verify different tasks around real-life and campus problems.

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-uac72b302)=

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u629041d6)=

![AlohaMini product description (for K12 education) · Figure 2](../_static/yuque-assets/ca9e2dc459ad60e26177.png)

- **Desktop operations**: Organize stationery, classify building blocks, place experimental materials, and accurately place items into designated containers.

- **Low pick-up**: Pick up items from the ground or a lower location, and then place them on the desktop, storage shelf or designated area.

- **Two-arm collaboration**: One robotic arm fixes the object, and the other robotic arm completes the operation; it can also continuously complete multi-step tasks such as "taking - moving - placing".

- **Mobile pick-and-place**: In a simulated supermarket, laboratory or campus service scenario, the robot moves autonomously to a designated area, identifies target items, and completes grabbing, transfer and delivery.

- **Scenario innovation**: Students can independently build scenarios based on campus life, such as "smart supermarkets", "experimental equipment sorting", "book delivery", "garbage classification", "material sorting", etc.

### Imitation learning: How does the AlohaMini learn to act autonomously?

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u8cb2eb53)=

**Imitation learning is currently one of the important methods for robots to acquire real operating capabilities.**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u12a26977)=

Unlike traditional robots that repeat actions according to a fixed program, imitation learning allows robots to learn tasks from human demonstrations.

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u15e7b4e7)=

It can be understood as the process of students learning a new skill:

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u15dc16cd)=

**First observe what the teacher does** **➡️** **Practice repeatedly** **➡️** **Complete independently**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u3408fed1)=

The same goes for robots:

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u4acf73b5)=

**Once a person demonstrates, the robot learns once; by continuously increasing real experience, the robot will have the opportunity to do the task better**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-ua8987826)=

AlohaMini helps students understand how robots can gradually move from being "controlled by humans" to "acting autonomously" through the following process:

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-ubc9f8519)=

**Shake operation demonstration → Data collection → Model training or inference → Real machine verification → Iterative optimization**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u15357e79)=

**Step One: Shake Operation Demonstration**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-uf38cbba4)=

Using a pair of handheld control arms (Leader Arms), students show the AlohaMini how to complete a task.

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u1e141ffc)=

This process is called "shake operation" or "teleoperation".

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u8b49ca1f)=

In layman’s terms, it is: **Students take the robot to complete a task and tell it “how this thing should be done.”**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u6b449833)=

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u0c6dfbc5)=

![AlohaMini product description (for K12 education) · Figure 4](../_static/yuque-assets/3ce0e488e6ddc561c9c2.png)

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u4482b70f)=

**Step 2: Data collection**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u069a5cf0)=

While students are demonstrating, AlohaMini will simultaneously record:

- How the robotic arm moves;

- When does the gripper grab and release items;

- What does the camera see?

- The sequential steps that a robot goes through while completing a task.

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-uc1ae3395)=

These records constitute the real data required for robot learning tasks, and are the robot’s experience of “what it saw and what it did” in the real environment.

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-ub6c100e7)=

**Step 3: Model Learning**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-uf9769579)=

Using the collected data, teachers and students can use imitation learning methods such as ACT to let the robot learn task rules.

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-uae1d257a)=

For example, when the robot sees the target product, the model will learn to judge: should the next step be to approach or grab it? How should I adjust the angle of the robotic arm? Where should it be moved after grabbing?

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-ud923fd8c)=

(Note: ACT is one of the commonly used imitation learning methods in the field of embodied intelligence. It is especially suitable for precise operation tasks such as grabbing and placing.)

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u823cfd4d)=

Hugging Face's LeRobot tool system uses ACT as one of the important entry methods for robot imitation learning. [LeRobot ACT Documentation](https://huggingface.co/docs/lerobot/act)

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u5ebb4cad)=

**Step 4: Real device verification**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-uaf56d8a8)=

After completing model training or inference, teachers and students can run the learning results on AlohaMini to observe whether the robot can autonomously complete similar tasks in real scenarios.

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u4c312414)=

For example, can the robot identify the target item? Is it possible to capture stably? Are you able to put items in the correct location?

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u90191b91)=

Students can intuitively see: where is the boundary of the model's capabilities, and how will the light, placement, object shape and operating path in the real environment affect the robot's performance?

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u7d58475f)=

**Step 5: Iterative optimization**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-ua5bab98a)=

If the robot fails to complete the task, students can analyze why:

- Are the number of demonstrations insufficient?

- Has the task environment changed too much?

- Did the camera see the target object clearly?

- Does the placement of items affect grabbing?

- Is it necessary to add new demonstration data?

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-uc8f5ab0a)=

In the process of supplementary demonstrations, environment adjustments and re-testing, students will experience a real engineering and scientific research process:

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-uae67654f)=

**Ask questions, record evidence, adjust plans, verify results,** **Continuous improvement** **.**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u272debed)=

**Through AlohaMini, students can start from "teaching the robot to complete a task" and further understand how data affects the model, how the model affects actions, and how to continuously improve the robot's task performance through real verification.**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u107a38fe)=

**In this way, embodied intelligence is no longer just an abstract concept, but a real process that students can personally participate in, observe, and study.**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u25985461)=

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-e5282ee3)=

### Full-stack ecological adaptation: How does AlohaMini continue to support cutting-edge embodied intelligence practices?

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u7e36da00)=

Embodied intelligence is still in a stage of rapid development, with new open source models, training methods, and development tools constantly emerging.

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u66b84701)=

**The value of AlohaMini lies not only in supporting current teaching tasks, but also in providing students with a real robotic platform that can continue to contact, understand and verify cutting-edge technologies.**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u761d0057)=

**AlohaMini is deeply compatible with the Hugging Face LeRobot open source tool system.**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-udcccf7d9)=

LeRobot can be understood as a set of open source "tool systems" for robot learning. It connects robot control, data collection, data management, model training, model inference and real machine testing, and provides a relatively unified usage method.

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u3d604e44)=

**This means that teachers and students do not need to build a complex software environment from scratch for each method, but can carry out learning, testing and innovation in the same practice link based on AlohaMini:**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-ud59676a1)=

**Task design → Shake operation demonstration → Data collection → Model training or inference → Real machine verification → Results improvement**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-ubd074911)=

Currently, AlohaMini supports the practice of mainstream imitation learning methods such as ACT, Diffusion Policy, and FastWAM. For beginners, methods such as ACT can help students understand how robots learn to complete tasks such as grabbing, placing, and sorting from human demonstrations.

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-ud06603ad)=

As the learning progresses, students can also compare the impact of different amounts of data, demonstration methods or learning methods on the robot's performance around the same task. For example:

- Can increasing the number of demonstrations improve the success rate of task completion?

- Can the robot continue to complete the task after changing the placement of the items?

- Are there differences in the performance of different models in grasping, sorting, or dual-arm collaboration tasks?

- How to improve scene design and training data to make robots complete tasks more stably?

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u499d9ab9)=

**These issues can become long-term projects for clubs, or they can be developed into the research content of high school research studies, scientific and technological innovation competitions or thesis papers.**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-ub5a06a4d)=

For teachers and students with advanced foundations, AlohaMini can also carry out reproduction, adaptation and real machine testing around open source embodied intelligent models such as Pi-0.5, SmolVLA, EO-1, VLA-JEPA, GR00T N1.7, and EVO1.

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-uad92c177)=

When a new open source model has open code, open interfaces, and meets corresponding software and hardware conditions, AlohaMini can be used as a real robot platform to support teachers and students in adaptation, testing, and verification.

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u8faa41bd)=

**Therefore, AlohaMini not only serves a fixed course or a fixed algorithm, but can also support embodied intelligence practices of different depths:**

- **For beginners**: Understand how robots learn from human demonstrations;

- **For clubs and competitions**: Data collection, model inference and debugging around real tasks;

- **Project-oriented research**: Compare the impact of data, models and task design on robot performance;

- **Facing frontier exploration**: When conditions are met, bring new open source methods to real robots for verification.

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u039819c4)=

**From learning existing methods to verifying new technical ideas; from a classroom experience to continuously accumulating course cases, student projects and research results, AlohaMini provides schools with a path to connect open source technology and real robot practice.**

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u0e380f0b)=

* Note: Different open source models have different requirements for computing resources, sensor configurations, software environments and task forms. The specific adaptation range is subject to actual conditions and official technical support plans. *

### Other advantages

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-ub76319ce)=

In addition to having real embodied intelligence development capabilities, AlohaMini also lowers the threshold for schools to carry out relevant practices through **high cost performance, safe design, robot delivery, and after-sales support**

#### ✅High cost performance

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-ub35a1965)=

The traditional "mobile chassis + lifting structure + double-arm collaboration" robot platform easily costs hundreds of thousands.

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-uc9546808)=

AlohaMini brings this complete configuration into the 10,000-yuan range, allowing schools to cost-effectively configure a real robot platform that can be used for shaking operations, data collection, model training, and real machine verification.

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-2d51f08f)=

#### ✅Safe design

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u84d5cae5)=

Unlike high-torque robots designed for industrial operations, high-speed motion or large-load tasks, AlohaMini uses a low-torque actuator design, which is more suitable for teaching practices such as shaking operations, data collection, and task testing under the guidance of teachers.

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-ua275346a)=

High-power, high-torque robots can complete higher-speed and larger-load movements, but may bring higher impact and risks when operating errors, collisions or abnormal control occur. AlohaMini takes human-computer interaction safety and equipment protection into teaching scenarios into design considerations, helping teachers and students conduct repeated explorations on real robots with greater peace of mind.

(yq-ixkt9xrygz0zv9b6-u4a8a7ad3)=

The equipment has two levels of joint stall protection:

- When the grippers encounters abnormal resistance, the system automatically removes the clamping force to reduce the risk of clamping overload;

- When the load on the robot arm joint exceeds the set range, the system stops outputting torque to reduce the risk of equipment overload and collision.
