### (1) Introduction to AlohaMini

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-u526f092f)=

AlohaMini is a mobile two-arm robot for embodied intelligent learning.

- **hardware advantages:** integrates an omnidirectional mobile chassis, an electric lifting module and a 6+1 degree of freedom aloha robotic arm. It can be adapted to a variety of unstructured practical tasks such as low-level ground picking, desktop operation, and long-arm trajectory coordination, and has strong scene coverage capabilities.

- **full-stack ecological adaptation:** is deeply compatible with the Hugging Face LeRobot v0.6 open source framework, opening up the entire link of teleoperation data set collection, model training, and local inference deployment; the communication link is targeted for high-frequency optimization, and natively supports the reproduction and fine-tuning of mainstream embodied algorithms:

- **imitation learning:** ACT, Diffusion Policy, FastWAM

- **VLA body model:** Pi-0.5, SmolVLA, EO-1, VLA-JEPA, GR00T N1.7, EVO1

1. In principle, the school will register uniformly, and direct registration from individual students will not be accepted.

1. Students need to participate in the selection and competition on the basis of their school or relevant organization.

1. The first prize team in the preliminary round is qualified to advance to the national finals.

1. In the embodied intelligence track, the top 90% of the first prize teams in the preliminary round will qualify for the national finals.

1. The organizing committee reserves a small number of co-ordinating quotas, and the details will be subject to the event notice of the current year.

1. **National Finals: July-August**

1. The offline national finals will be held in Shanghai.

1. Participating teams must complete on-site robot tasks within the specified time and receive evaluation of task results and on-site performance.

## 2. Core advantages

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-ce457c0b)=

### Frontier: Reproducing Frontier Algorithms

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-u8ac50419)=

The course focuses on imitation learning, an important technical route in the field of embodied intelligence. Students will not only learn traditional rule programming or remote control operation, but also understand how robots gradually gain the ability to complete tasks through human demonstration, data collection, and model learning.

- Understand the robot structure, sensors, actuators and control systems.

- Use the Leader arm to complete teleoperation tasks.

- Learn how teleoperation demonstrations translate into images, robot status, and motion data.

- Learn to judge data quality and understand the impact of data on model performance.

- Understand the relationship between model learning and autonomous robot execution.

- Experience the complete technical path of "human demonstration-data collection-model learning-autonomous execution" in the advanced course.

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-ac4f415d)=

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-u59ca96ea)=

AlohaMini reduces the equipment investment of hundreds of thousands of yuan for traditional dual-arm robots to a level of 10,000 yuan that is more suitable for educational scenarios. ### Safety: low torque joint + two-level stall protection

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-u3352e289)=

Compared with some humanoid robots that are large in size, have high joint torque and fast movement speed, AlohaMini is more suitable for close observation, operation and project teaching in educational scenes.

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-u284ecc7d)=

Large humanoid robots usually require stricter isolation space, operating permissions and professional supervision;

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-ue0189122)=

AlohaMini focuses on the design of low-torque actuators and two-stage stall protection, making it easier for students to participate under the guidance of teachers.

- Adopt low-torque actuator design to reduce the risk of mechanical collision during classroom operations.

- Equipped with two-level joint stall protection: when the current of the clamping claw exceeds 500mA, the clamping force is automatically released; when the current of other robot arm joints reaches 1800mA, the torque output is stopped, reducing the risk of clamping, stalling and equipment overload.

- The course is equipped with fixed safety regulations, including work area boundary management, pre-class equipment inspection, startup and reset procedures, abnormal stop requirements and full teacher supervision.

- Students operate in a team collaboration mode of 2-4 people, with one person controlling, multiple people observing and safety supervision, taking into account both learning participation and classroom safety.

## 3. Course details

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-u0e777a06)=

This course focuses on **gradient** **layered teaching** **, special empowerment of official competitions, and first-line enterprise study** The combined model gradually consolidates students' embodied intelligence professional knowledge and practical abilities, comprehensively cultivates comprehensive scientific and technological literacy, and builds a one-stop scientific and technological growth channel for students from entry-level advancement, competition sprint to industrial awareness.

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-08fd9b2c)=

### Hierarchical teaching system

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-u24338717)=

Gradient course content is set up for entry-level students, students with advanced abilities, and top-level competition students, taking into account universal teaching and elite competition training, accurately matching the ability levels of students at different stages, and achieving step-by-step improvement.

- **Basic layer (zero-based entry)**: Focus on the basic cognition of embodied intelligence, systematically learn the structural principles of robots and operating safety regulations, master basic practical skills such as Leader robotic arm assembly and teleoperation control, and build a basic framework for subject knowledge and practical capabilities.

- **Improvement level (advanced capabilities)**: Focus on polishing comprehensive abilities, delving into core contents such as robot task design, data collection, and data quality screening, and simultaneously cultivating students' comprehensive qualities in project sorting, achievement display, and logical expression.

- **Advancement (elevating the competition)**: Connect the industry’s cutting-edge algorithms and embodied intelligence technology systems, focus on the National Youth Artificial Intelligence Competition (whitelist event) embodied intelligence track for special training, carry out targeted optimization of event tasks, and strive for high-level event results.

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-u60f51bcd)=

At the same time, a normalized transition mechanism for further studies has been established. Students in regular classes in the spring and autumn can smoothly connect to the selection class, preliminary training, semi-finals and finals training through layer-by-layer selection, forming a closed-loop talent training channel.

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-d0ccc545)=

#### (1) Regular courses in spring and autumn

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-sKB0D)=

##### 1. Course introduction

- **AlohaMini Embodied Intelligence Interest Course**

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-u0974db03)=

Relying on the AlohaMini mobile dual-arm robot, a 16-hour regular embodied intelligence course in spring and autumn is launched. The course has one class period per week, each class period is 60 minutes, and students with no basic knowledge can also participate in the study.

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-uddaa8e11)=

The course starts from the understanding of the robot industry, Aloha Mini structure and safety specifications, and gradually carries out popular science on Leader arm assembly, teleoperation, task challenges, data collection, data quality judgment and imitation learning. Students will understand the embodied intelligence technology closed loop of "human demonstration - data collection - model learning - robot autonomous execution" on a real robot platform, and complete group project design and results presentation.

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-u62f09ccb)=

Different from traditional robotics courses that focus on building blocks, fixed programming or simple remote control, this course allows students to come into contact with real mobile dual-arm robots, real tasks and real data, and build an intuitive understanding of current embodied intelligence technology and engineering practice capabilities.

- **Can be learned with zero basic knowledge**

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-uf586b23d)=

The course adopts a group collaboration model of 2-4 people, with each student taking turns taking on roles such as operation, assembly, safety, recording, and explanation.

- Students with zero foundation can start with robot cognition, structural observation, basic assembly and teleoperation tasks, and gradually build up interest and operational confidence.

- Students with competition potential can enter subsequent selection and special training to strengthen task dismantling, operational stability, teamwork and on-site demonstration capabilities.

- **Connected to the National Youth Artificial Intelligence Competition**

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-ucb087bb0)=

Aloha Mini has been used as the machine for the finals of the Embodied Intelligent Track of the first National Youth Artificial Intelligence Competition. The spring and autumn courses focus on basic training of the core abilities required for this type of competition, including robot structure cognition, teleoperation control, real task execution, data understanding, project design and team presentation.

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-uc58e16e3)=

After the course, students can be selected based on their operational ability, project performance, collaboration ability and expression ability to enter the preliminary special training. The preliminary competition course will focus on task understanding, time-limited operations, on-site debugging, project display and simulated competition training based on the official competition questions and rules of the year.

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-ude159dea)=

Among them, senior primary school students mainly focus on embodied intelligence enlightenment, engineering practice and ability reserve; middle and high school students can further connect competition selection and preparation training, forming a continuous path from interest cultivation to competition advancement.

- **Course Outcomes**

(yq-us0tnge3dszgxb69-udc7a5c75)=

Upon completion of spring and fall courses, each group will form:
