(yq-km9oz425a6h1307w-ub2e501b1)=

### Case 4

(yq-km9oz425a6h1307w-uf0cdb953)=

Overseas maker Histochemichael has developed a teleoperation solution for AlohaMini: the operator can use 4 foot pedals to control the forward and backward movement of the chassis and the lifting and lowering of the robot, control the steering of the chassis through facial tracking through a computer camera, and use the So101 main operating arm to remotely and synchronously control the movements of AlohaMini's arms.
